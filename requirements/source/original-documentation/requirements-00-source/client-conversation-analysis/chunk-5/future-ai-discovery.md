# Future & AI Discovery - Chunk 5

* **Customer Churn Prediction**: AI to predict if a regular customer is likely to stop ordering based on order frequency, quantity drops, payment issues, or complaints. [FUTURE]
* **Order Pattern Forecasting**: AI to learn dealer order patterns (e.g., specific day volumes) and automatically suggest order drafts. [FUTURE]
* **Backup Supplier Recommendation**: AI to automatically recommend alternative suppliers if the primary supplier fails or is delayed. [FUTURE]
* **Fraud / Suspicious Transaction Detection**: AI to detect unusual patterns like repeated stock adjustments, unusual discounts, or abnormal returns by a specific user. [FUTURE]
* **Business Health Score**: A unified AI-driven score (e.g., 82/100) aggregating multiple KPIs (Production, Sales, Inventory, Finance, Quality, Forecast) to represent overall business health. [FUTURE]
* **Vehicle Maintenance Prediction**: AI to recommend vehicle maintenance not just based on due date, but also usage, past repairs, and breakdown frequency. [FUTURE]
* **Environmental Root Cause Analysis**: AI to link environmental IoT sensor data (temperature, humidity) with farm performance metrics (mortality, weight gain) to explain anomalies. [FUTURE]
