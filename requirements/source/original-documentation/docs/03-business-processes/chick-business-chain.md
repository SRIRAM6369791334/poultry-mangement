# Chick Business Chain (Poultry ERP)

## 1. Overview
The chick business chain maps the lifecycle from the incubation of hatching eggs to the production of Day-Old Chicks (DOCs), and their subsequent placement on farms to grow into broilers or future layers/breeders.

## 2. Hatchery Operations (Production of DOCs)
### A. Egg Receiving & Storage
- **Receipt**: Hatching eggs arrive from breeder farms.
- **Storage**: Kept in cold rooms (15-18°C) until setting time.

### B. Incubation (Setters)
- **Setting**: Eggs placed in incubator trays.
- **Process**: Temperature, humidity, and turning are tightly controlled.
- **Duration**: Days 1 to 18 (for chickens).

### C. Candling & Transfer
- **Candling (Day 18)**: Eggs scanned to remove infertile eggs and dead embryos (clears).
- **Transfer**: Viable eggs transferred from setter trays to hatcher baskets.

### D. Hatching (Hatchers)
- **Duration**: Days 19 to 21.
- **Hatch Day**: Chicks emerge from shells.
- **Take-off**: Chicks are removed from hatchers.

### E. Processing & Grading
- **Grading**: Culling of weak, deformed, or unhealed chicks.
- **Sexing**: (Optional/Layer specific) Separating males from females.
- **Vaccination**: Administering day-old vaccines (e.g., Marek's disease) via spray or subcutaneous injection.
- **Boxing**: Packed in chick boxes (usually 100 chicks + 2% extra for early mortality).

## 3. Sales & Dispatch of DOCs
- **Order Management**: Pre-booked orders by contract farmers or independent buyers.
- **Dispatch**: Loaded into climate-controlled chick vans.
- **Documentation**: Health certificates, delivery challan, and invoice generated.
- **Transit**: High priority, time-sensitive transport to prevent dehydration.

## 4. Farm Placement (Growing Phase)
### A. Broiler Chain
- **Placement**: DOCs placed in temperature-controlled brooding rings.
- **Growing Phase**: 35-45 days.
- **Inputs**: Starter, Grower, and Finisher feed, vaccines, medicines, water.
- **Harvest**: Birds reach target weight (e.g., 2.0 - 2.5 kg).
- **Sales/Processing**: Birds sold alive via traders or sent to a processing plant to become dressed meat.

### B. Layer/Breeder Rearing Chain
- **Placement**: DOCs placed in rearing sheds.
- **Rearing Phase**: 0-16 weeks.
- **Inputs**: Specialized rearing feeds, extensive vaccination program, light management.
- **Transfer**: At point-of-lay (16-18 weeks), moved to production sheds.

## 5. Key Metrics & KPIs
- **Hatchability**: % of total eggs that hatch into viable chicks.
- **Cull Rate**: % of chicks rejected at grading.
- **First Week Mortality**: Critical indicator of chick quality and brooding management.
- **Broiler FCR**: kg feed consumed / kg live weight gained.
- **Livability**: % of birds surviving to harvest/transfer.
