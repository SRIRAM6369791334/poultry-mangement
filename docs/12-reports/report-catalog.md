# Report Catalog (AG-12)

This catalog outlines 50+ standard reports available in the Poultry Management ERP system.

## 1. Farm & Flock Reports
* **REP-1001: Active Batch Status** - Real-time snapshot of all active batches across farms (Age, Size, Mortality, FCR).
* **REP-1002: Batch History Report** - Comprehensive lifecycle data of completed batches.
* **REP-1003: Farm Performance Ranking** - Ranks farms based on FCR, PEF (Poultry Efficiency Factor), and cost per kg.
* **REP-1004: Shed Utilization Report** - Tracks empty days vs occupied days per shed.
* **REP-1005: Daily Farm Operations Log** - Audit trail of all daily data entries (feed, water, temp) per batch.

## 2. Production Reports (Broiler)
* **REP-2001: Broiler Daily Growth Report** - Actual vs Standard Body Weight (BW) and Average Daily Gain (ADG).
* **REP-2002: Broiler Mortality Analysis** - Daily and cumulative mortality %, with standard deviation.
* **REP-2003: FCR Trend Analysis** - Feed Conversion Ratio trends over the batch lifecycle.
* **REP-2004: End of Batch Summary (Liquidation)** - Final metrics: Livability, FCR, Average Weight, PEF, Cost per kg.
* **REP-2005: Catching & Dispatch Variance** - Farm weight vs Weighbridge weight analysis.

## 3. Production Reports (Layer/Breeder)
* **REP-2101: Daily Egg Production Report** - Hen-Day Production (HDP) %, Hen-Housed Production (HHP).
* **REP-2102: Egg Grading Summary** - Breakdown of commercial, hatching, jumbo, peewee, cracked, and dirty eggs.
* **REP-2103: Breeder Hatchability Report** - Total eggs set vs chicks hatched, fertility %, hatchability %.
* **REP-2104: Breeder Body Weight Uniformity** - Flock uniformity percentage and coefficient of variation (CV).
* **REP-2105: Egg Stock Ledger** - Daily opening, collection, sales, and closing stock of eggs.

## 4. Production Reports (Hatchery)
* **REP-2201: Setter Loading Report** - Details of eggs loaded, source flock, and expected hatch date.
* **REP-2202: Candling Report** - Infertility and early embryonic death percentages.
* **REP-2203: Hatchery Output & Cull Report** - Saleable chicks, weak chicks, culls, and unhatched eggs.
* **REP-2204: Hatchability Variance Analysis** - Actual vs expected hatchability by flock source.
* **REP-2205: Chick Dispatch Report** - Daily chick sales and transport details.

## 5. Feed Reports
* **REP-3001: Feed Consumption vs Standard** - Actual daily feed intake (g/bird) vs breed standard curve.
* **REP-3002: Feed Stock Ledger** - Opening, inwards, issues, and closing balance of feed bags.
* **REP-3003: Feed Mill Production Log** - Daily feed manufactured by formula.
* **REP-3004: Raw Material Usage Variance** - Actual ingredients consumed vs BOM (Bill of Materials) standard.
* **REP-3005: Feed Cost Analysis** - Cost per ton of feed produced based on current ingredient prices.

## 6. Health & Vaccination Reports
* **REP-4001: Vaccination Compliance Report** - Scheduled vs Actual vaccinations by batch.
* **REP-4002: Disease Outbreak Map** - Incidents of diseases across farms/sheds.
* **REP-4003: Post-Mortem Analysis** - Summary of lesions and causes of death.
* **REP-4004: Medication Usage Report** - Cost and quantity of antibiotics/vitamins consumed per batch.
* **REP-4005: Water Quality & Sanitation Log** - Records of water sanitization and testing results.

## 7. Inventory Reports
* **REP-5001: Inventory Valuation Report** - Current value of all stock (FIFO/Moving Average).
* **REP-5002: Reorder Level Alert Report** - Items below minimum stock threshold.
* **REP-5003: Stock Expiry Report** - Medicines and vaccines expiring within 30/60/90 days.
* **REP-5004: Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Register** - Log of all inward shipments.
* **REP-5005: Material Issue Register** - Log of all materials issued to farms/sheds.
* **REP-5006: Stock Reconciliation Report** - Physical vs system stock variances.

## 8. Financial Reports
* **REP-6001: Batch Profit & Loss (BPL)** - Comprehensive income and expenses for a specific batch.
* **REP-6002: Farm/Branch Profitability** - P&L aggregated by farm or branch.
* **REP-6003: Cost of Production (COP)** - Detailed breakdown of cost per kg or cost per egg.
* **REP-6004: Trial Balance** - Standard accounting TB.
* **REP-6005: General Ledger** - Detailed account transactions.
* **REP-6006: Accounts Payable Aging** - Outstanding vendor payments.
* **REP-6007: Accounts Receivable Aging** - Outstanding customer payments.
* **REP-6008: Cash Flow Statement** - Inflows and outflows.

## 9. Sales Reports
* **REP-7001: Daily Sales Register** - All invoices generated in a day (Birds, Eggs, Feed).
* **REP-7002: Customer Outstanding Summary** - Due balances and credit limits by customer.
* **REP-7003: Sales Realization Report** - Average selling price achieved per kg/unit vs market rate.
* **REP-7004: Dispatch Variance Report** - Requested qty vs Dispatched qty vs Delivered qty.
* **REP-7005: Customer Profitability Analysis** - Margin earned per customer.

## 10. Purchase Reports
* **REP-8001: Purchase Order Register** - Status of all POs (Pending, Fulfilled, Canceled).
* **REP-8002: Vendor Performance Report** - Delivery time, quality rejections, and price variations.
* **REP-8003: Price Trend Analysis** - Historical price trends of key inputs (Maize, Soya, DOC).
* **REP-8004: Pending Indent Report** - Requisitions waiting for PO creation.

## 11. HR & Management Reports
* **REP-9001: Employee Attendance & Overtime** - Time logs for payroll.
* **REP-9002: Labor Productivity** - E.g., Eggs collected per worker hour.
* **REP-9003: Executive Dashboard Summary** - Consolidated PDF of high-level KPIs.
* **REP-9004: Audit Trail Report** - System log of data modifications/deletions.

*All reports support export to PDF, Excel, and CSV, and can be scheduled for email delivery via the Report Scheduler module.*
