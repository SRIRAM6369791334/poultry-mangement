# Complete Poultry Industry Glossary

This glossary contains essential terminology used across all aspects of the poultry industry. It is designed to serve as a domain knowledge base for the ERP system development.

*Total Terms: 164*

## Production Terms
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio)** | Ratio of feed consumed to weight gained (or eggs produced). | Used to measure feed efficiency. | Feed Intake, ADG |
| **ADG (Average Daily Gain)** | Average amount of weight a bird gains per day. | Crucial metric in broiler farming. | Body Weight, FCR |
| **Livability** | Percentage of birds that survive to the end of the production cycle. | General measure of flock health. | Mortality |
| **Mortality** | Percentage of birds that die during the cycle. | Key performance indicator. | Livability, Cull Rate |
| **Uniformity** | Percentage of birds within +/- 10% of the average body weight. | Determines flock consistency and processing efficiency. | CV, Body Weight |
| **Hen-Day Production** | Number of eggs produced divided by the number of hens alive on that day. | Daily layer performance metric. | Hen-Housed Production |
| **Hen-Housed Production** | Number of eggs produced divided by the initial number of hens placed. | Long-term layer profitability metric. | Hen-Day Production |
| **Hatchability** | Percentage of eggs that successfully hatch. | Key hatchery metric. | Fertility, HOSE, HOFE |
| **Fertility** | Percentage of eggs that are fertilized. | Key breeder metric. | Hatchability, Mating Ratio |
| **EPEF (European Production Efficiency Factor)** | Standardized formula combining mortality, body weight, FCR, and age. | Used to benchmark broiler flock performance internationally. | PI |
| **PI (Performance Index)** | Similar to EPEF, a mathematical score of flock performance. | Used for benchmarking. | EPEF |
| **CV (Coefficient of Variation)** | Statistical measure of weight variation in a flock. | Lower CV means better uniformity. | Uniformity |
| **Peak Production** | The highest rate of egg laying a flock achieves (usually ~90-95%). | Critical milestone in layer/breeder cycles. | Persistency |
| **Persistency** | The ability of a flock to maintain high egg production after peak. | Determines total lifecycle profitability. | Peak Production |
| **Body Weight (BW)** | The physical weight of the bird. | Tracked weekly against standard breeder curves. | ADG, Uniformity |
| **Feed Intake** | Amount of feed consumed per bird per day. | Used to calculate FCR. | Water Intake |
| **Water Intake** | Amount of water consumed per bird per day. | Indicator of health; typically 1.6-2.0 times feed intake. | Feed Intake, Water to Feed Ratio |
| **Water to Feed Ratio** | The ratio of water consumed to feed consumed. | Sudden changes indicate disease or temperature stress. | Water Intake |
| **Egg Weight** | The physical weight of an individual egg. | Determines egg grading and pricing. | Egg Mass |
| **Egg Mass** | Total weight of eggs produced by a flock per day (Hen-Day % * Egg Weight). | True measure of layer nutrient output. | Egg Weight |
| **Shell Thickness** | Measurement of eggshell quality. | Thinner shells lead to higher crack rates. | Specific Gravity |
| **Yolk Color** | Measurement of yolk pigmentation (often using Roche color fan). | Market preference dictates desired color. | Layer Mash |
| **Haugh Unit** | Measure of egg protein quality based on albumen height. | Higher value indicates fresher, higher quality egg. | Egg Grading |
| **Cull Rate** | Percentage of birds intentionally removed due to sickness or poor performance. | Separate from natural mortality. | Mortality, Depletion |
| **DOA (Dead on Arrival)** | Birds that die during transport to the farm or processing plant. | Logistics and welfare metric. | Shrinkage |

## Bird Types & Genetics
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Broiler** | A chicken bred specifically for meat production. | Standard meat bird. | Heavy Broiler |
| **Layer** | A chicken bred specifically for commercial egg production. | Standard egg bird. | Pullet, Spent Hen |
| **Breeder** | A chicken kept to produce fertile hatching eggs. | Source of future generations. | PS, GPS |
| **Pullet** | A young female chicken before she starts laying eggs (usually 0-16 weeks). | Rearing phase terminology. | Point of Lay |
| **Cockerel** | A young male chicken. | Breeder male terminology. | Rooster, Sire |
| **Rooster (Cock)** | An adult male chicken. | Breeder male. | Cockerel |
| **Cornish Hen** | A very young, small broiler chicken slaughtered early. | Niche market meat product. | Broiler |
| **Day-Old Chick (DOC)** | A newly hatched chick, usually within 24 hours of hatching. | Starting point for farms. | Placement |
| **Parent Stock (PS)** | The birds that produce the commercial broilers or layers. | Standard breeder farm bird. | GPS, Commercial |
| **Grandparent Stock (GPS)** | The birds that produce the Parent Stock. | High biosecurity genetic stock. | GGP, PS |
| **Great Grandparent (GGP)** | The pure lines used to produce Grandparent stock. | Owned exclusively by primary breeding companies. | GPS |
| **Spent Hen** | A layer or breeder hen that has finished her productive laying cycle. | Sold for low-value meat or rendering. | Cull |
| **Cull (Bird)** | An unproductive or sick bird removed from the flock. | Action taken to improve flock efficiency. | Spent Hen |
| **Point of Lay (POL) Pullet** | A pullet (usually 16-18 weeks old) ready to start laying eggs. | Often sold from rearing farms to layer farms. | Pullet, Layer |
| **Commercial Layer** | A bird laying eggs for human consumption (not for hatching). | Standard table egg producer. | Layer, Breeder |
| **Sire** | The male parent. | Genetics/Breeder context. | Dam |
| **Dam** | The female parent. | Genetics/Breeder context. | Sire |
| **Straight Run** | An unsexed flock of chicks containing both males and females. | Common in standard broiler farming. | As Hatched |
| **Sexed Chick** | Chicks that have been separated by gender at the hatchery. | Standard for layers and breeders. | Vent Sexing, Feather Sexing |
| **As Hatched** | Chicks sold without sex separation (same as Straight Run). | Broiler terminology. | Straight Run |
| **Heavy Broiler** | A broiler grown to a larger weight (e.g., 2.5kg+) for deboning/processing. | Processing plant requirement. | Broiler |
| **Light Layer** | A breed of layer optimized for minimal feed intake and high egg output. | Standard commercial layer type. | Layer |

## Housing & Environment
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shed / House** | The physical building where birds are housed. | General farm infrastructure. | Environmentally Controlled House |
| **Cage System** | Housing where birds are kept in wire enclosures. | Common for commercial layers. | Battery Cage, Enriched Cage |
| **Deep Litter** | Housing system where birds are kept on the floor covered with bedding material. | Standard for broilers and breeders. | Litter, Slats |
| **Slats** | Raised flooring (plastic or wood) that allows manure to fall through. | Used in breeder houses (often slat-and-litter combination). | Deep Litter |
| **Environmentally Controlled (EC) House** | A closed house with automated ventilation, cooling, and heating. | Modern, high-efficiency farming. | Open Sided House |
| **Open Sided House** | A house with open sides (often covered by curtains) relying on natural ventilation. | Common in tropical/developing regions. | EC House |
| **Brooder House** | A specialized house for rearing chicks in their first few weeks. | Requires high heating capacity. | Rearing House |
| **Rearing House** | A house used to grow pullets until Point of Lay. | Layer/Breeder lifecycle. | Laying House |
| **Laying House** | A house equipped with nest boxes or cages for egg production. | Adult layer/breeder phase. | Rearing House |
| **Breeder House** | A house designed for mating, typically with a scratch area and nest boxes. | Parent stock phase. | Slats, Nest Box |
| **Free Range** | A system where birds have access to the outdoors. | Niche/Premium market requirement. | Aviary System |
| **Aviary System** | A multi-tier cage-free indoor housing system for layers. | Welfare-friendly alternative to cages. | Free Range, Cage System |
| **Battery Cage** | Traditional small wire cages for layers (banned in some regions). | High density, older system. | Enriched Cage |
| **Enriched Cage** | A larger cage with amenities like a nest pad, scratch mat, and perches. | Meets certain welfare standards. | Battery Cage |
| **Nipple Drinker Line** | A closed water pipe system with valves (nipples) that birds peck to drink. | Standard modern watering system. | Plasson Drinker |
| **Feeder Line** | An automated system (often auger/pan) delivering feed throughout the house. | Automated feeding. | Pan Feeder |
| **Plasson Drinker** | A bell-shaped open waterer suspended from the ceiling. | Older or open-sided house watering system. | Nipple Drinker |
| **Nest Box** | An enclosed space for hens to lay eggs (manual or automated collection). | Breeder and cage-free layer systems. | Roll-away Nest |
| **Scratch Area** | Litter area in a breeder house where birds can mate and forage. | Breeder housing design. | Slats |
| **Litter** | Bedding material used on the floor (wood shavings, rice husks). | Absorbs moisture and dilutes feces. | Deep Litter |
| **Wood Shavings** | Common litter material made from softwoods. | Bedding. | Litter |
| **Rice Husks** | Common litter material in Asia. | Bedding. | Litter |
| **Curtain** | Adjustable side covers for open houses to control airflow and temperature. | Open-sided housing. | EC House |

## Feed & Nutrition
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter Feed** | High-protein feed given to chicks in their first 1-2 weeks. | Promotes rapid early growth. | Grower, Pre-Starter |
| **Grower Feed** | Medium-protein feed given during the main growth phase. | Transition feed. | Starter, Finisher |
| **Finisher Feed** | High-energy, lower-protein feed given prior to slaughter. | Maximizes final weight gain. | Grower, Withdrawal Feed |
| **Pre-Starter** | Highly specialized, easily digestible feed for the first 3-5 days. | Premium broiler nutrition. | Starter |
| **Layer Mash** | Feed formulated for producing hens, high in calcium. | Commercial egg production. | Breeder Ration |
| **Pellet** | Feed compressed into hard cylinders. | Improves feed intake and reduces waste in broilers. | Crumble, Mash |
| **Crumble** | Pelleted feed that has been crushed into smaller pieces. | Used for younger birds that cannot eat full pellets. | Pellet, Mash |
| **Mash** | Loosely mixed, uncompressed feed ingredients. | Standard for layers and breeders. | Pellet |
| **Withdrawal Feed** | Feed without medications/coccidiostats given just before slaughter. | Ensures no drug residue in meat. | Finisher |
| **Breeder Ration** | Specialized feed for parent stock, balanced for fertility and hatchability. | Breeder management. | Layer Mash |
| **Spike Feeding** | Temporary increase in feed given to breeder males to boost mating activity. | Breeder management. | Male Ration |
| **Ad Libitum** | Allowing birds unlimited access to feed and water. | Standard for broilers. | Restricted Feeding |
| **Restricted Feeding** | Controlling feed intake to prevent obesity (essential for breeders). | Breeder management. | Ad Libitum |
| **Phase Feeding** | Changing feed formulations multiple times to match exact bird age/needs. | Precision nutrition. | Formulation |
| **Calcium Grit** | Large particles of calcium (oyster shell/limestone) added to layer diets. | Improves late-night shell formation. | Layer Mash |
| **Premix** | A concentrated blend of vitamins and minerals added to feed. | Feed mill ingredient. | Concentrate |
| **Concentrate** | A mix of proteins, vitamins, and minerals; needs only grain added. | Used by farmers mixing their own feed. | Premix |
| **Soybean Meal** | Primary protein source in poultry diets. | Feed formulation. | Fish Meal |
| **Fish Meal** | High-quality animal protein source. | Feed formulation. | Soybean Meal |
| **Mycotoxin Binder** | Additive used to neutralize toxins from moldy grains. | Feed safety. | Aflatoxin |
| **Coccidiostat** | Medication in feed to prevent coccidiosis. | Health management. | Withdrawal Feed |
| **Probiotic** | Beneficial bacteria added to feed/water to improve gut health. | Alternative to antibiotics. | Prebiotic |
| **Prebiotic** | Fibers/compounds that feed beneficial gut bacteria. | Gut health. | Probiotic |
| **Grit** | Small stones eaten by birds to help their gizzard grind food. | Digestive aid (free range/mash diets). | Calcium Grit |

## Health & Biosecurity
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Newcastle Disease (ND)** | A highly contagious viral disease causing respiratory and nervous signs. | Critical biosecurity threat. | Vaccination |
| **Marek's Disease** | A viral disease causing tumors and paralysis. | Prevented via in-ovo or day-1 vaccination. | Vaccination |
| **IBD (Gumboro)** | Infectious Bursal Disease; destroys the immune system of young chicks. | Major viral disease. | Vaccination |
| **Coccidiosis** | A parasitic disease of the intestinal tract. | Controlled via feed additives (coccidiostats) or vaccines. | Coccidiostat, Necrotic Enteritis |
| **Aflatoxicosis** | Poisoning caused by aflatoxins in moldy feed. | Causes liver damage and immunosuppression. | Mycotoxin Binder |
| **Fowl Pox** | A viral disease causing skin lesions (dry pox) or respiratory lesions (wet pox). | Controlled via wing-web vaccination. | Vaccination |
| **Infectious Bronchitis (IB)** | A highly contagious coronavirus causing respiratory issues and egg drop. | Major layer/breeder concern. | Vaccination |
| **Avian Influenza (AI)** | Bird flu; highly pathogenic strains cause devastating mortality. | Notifiable disease; causes international trade bans. | Biosecurity |
| **Mycoplasma (MG/MS)** | Bacterial infection causing chronic respiratory disease or synovitis. | Flocks are often tested to be "Mycoplasma free". | CRD |
| **Salmonella** | Bacterial infection; major food safety concern for humans. | Strict testing protocols required. | Biosecurity |
| **E. coli** | Opportunistic bacteria causing colibacillosis. | Often secondary to viral infections or poor environment. | Mortality |
| **CRD (Chronic Respiratory Disease)** | Respiratory syndrome often triggered by Mycoplasma and E. coli. | Common in poorly ventilated houses. | Mycoplasma |
| **Coryza** | Bacterial infection causing facial swelling and nasal discharge. | Reduces egg production. | Vaccination |
| **Necrotic Enteritis** | Severe bacterial gut infection, often secondary to coccidiosis. | Causes sudden mortality. | Coccidiosis |
| **Biosecurity** | Procedures preventing the introduction and spread of disease (showers, tire dips). | Foundation of farm health. | Isolation |
| **Vaccination** | Administration of a vaccine to stimulate immunity. | Core preventative health practice. | Titer, Live Vaccine |
| **Titer** | A laboratory measure of antibody levels in the blood. | Used to check if vaccination was successful. | Vaccination |
| **Live Vaccine** | A vaccine containing a weakened living virus. | Triggers strong, fast immunity. | Killed Vaccine |
| **Killed Vaccine** | An inactivated virus injected into the bird. | Provides long-lasting immunity (often used in breeders/layers). | Live Vaccine |
| **In-Ovo Vaccination** | Injecting vaccine directly into the egg at day 18 of incubation. | Modern hatchery automation. | Marek's Disease |
| **Spray Vaccination** | Spraying DOCs with vaccine droplets at the hatchery. | Common for IB and ND. | Water Vaccination |
| **Water Vaccination** | Mixing vaccine into the drinking water at the farm. | Mass administration method. | Vaccination |
| **Withdrawal Period** | The time required after medication before a bird/egg can be sold for human consumption. | Food safety compliance. | Withdrawal Feed |

## Business & Processing
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Batch / Flock** | A group of birds of the same age raised together. | Primary unit of tracking in ERP. | Placement |
| **Placement** | The act of receiving DOCs and putting them into a house. | Start of the flock cycle. | Depletion |
| **Depletion** | The process of removing birds from the house (selling/slaughter). | End of the broiler cycle. | Placement, Culling |
| **Culling (Business)** | The process of removing an entire flock at the end of its life. | Layer/Breeder cycle end. | Spent Hen |
| **Integrator** | A company that owns the supply chain (chicks, feed, processing) and contracts growers. | Dominant business model in broilers. | Contract Grower |
| **Contract Grower** | An independent farmer who grows birds for an integrator. | Asset-light farming model. | Integrator |
| **Buy-back Rate** | The agreed price at which an integrator buys birds back from a grower. | Contract farming finance. | Growing Fee |
| **Growing Fee** | The payment made to a contract grower per kg or per bird. | Determines grower income. | Settlement |
| **Settlement** | The final financial calculation paying a grower based on performance (FCR, Mortality). | Critical ERP function. | Growing Fee |
| **Flock Cycle** | The complete lifespan of a flock on a farm. | Determines farm throughput. | Downtime |
| **Downtime** | The empty period between flocks used for cleaning and disinfection. | Crucial for breaking disease cycles. | AIAO |
| **All-In All-Out (AIAO)** | Management practice where all birds in a house arrive and leave on the same day. | Best practice for biosecurity. | Downtime |
| **Shrinkage** | Weight lost by live birds during transport to the slaughterhouse. | Affects final payout. | DOA, Live Weight |
| **Condemnation Rate** | Percentage of carcasses rejected at the slaughterhouse due to disease/defects. | Reduces payable weight. | Processing |
| **Grade A** | A perfect carcass with no bruises, broken bones, or defects. | Premium retail product. | Grade B |
| **Grade B** | A carcass with minor defects, often downgraded to cut-up or processed parts. | Lower value product. | Grade A |
| **Live Weight** | The weight of the bird alive on the farm or scale. | Basis for farmer payment. | Dressed Weight |
| **Dressed Weight** | The weight of the carcass after feathers, blood, and offal are removed. | Processing metric. | Carcass Yield |
| **Carcass Yield** | The percentage of live weight that remains as saleable meat. | Crucial processing profitability metric. | Live Weight, Dressed Weight |
| **Giblets** | The edible offal (heart, liver, gizzard, neck). | Sold separately or stuffed in whole birds. | Processing |
| **Processing** | The act of slaughtering and preparing meat for consumption. | Value-add step. | Slaughterhouse |
| **Slaughterhouse / Abattoir** | The facility where processing occurs. | Supply chain node. | Processing |
| **Rendering** | Processing waste (feathers, blood, offal) into by-products like meat/bone meal. | Waste management/cost recovery. | Slaughterhouse |
| **Traceability** | The ability to track meat/eggs back to the specific parent flock, feed batch, and farm. | Food safety requirement. | Batch |

## Equipment & Infrastructure
| Term | Definition | Context | Related Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pan Feeder** | A circular automated feed dispenser. | Standard broiler equipment. | Feeder Line |
| **Drinker (Bell/Nipple)** | Equipment dispensing water. | Water management. | Nipple Drinker Line |
| **Cool Pad** | Evaporative cooling pads placed at air inlets to drop house temperature. | EC House equipment. | Tunnel Ventilation |
| **Tunnel Ventilation** | Using large exhaust fans to pull air at high velocity through the house like a wind tunnel. | Summer cooling strategy. | Cool Pad, Exhaust Fan |
| **Exhaust Fan** | Large fans used to pull air out of the house. | EC House. | Tunnel Ventilation |
| **Brooder** | A localized heater (gas or electric) used for chicks. | Early rearing. | Brooder House |
| **Silo / Bin** | Outdoor storage tank for bulk feed. | Feed logistics. | Auger |
| **Auger** | A rotating screw mechanism that moves feed from the silo into the house. | Automated feeding. | Silo, Feeder Line |
| **Weighbridge** | Large scale used to weigh trucks (feed trucks, live bird trucks). | Logistics/Accounting. | Settlement |
| **Incubator / Setter** | Machine that holds eggs for the first 18 days under precise heat/humidity/turning. | Hatchery. | Hatcher |
| **Hatcher** | Machine where eggs are transferred for the final 3 days to hatch. | Hatchery. | Incubator |
| **Egg Candler** | Light source used to inspect eggs for fertility or cracks. | Hatchery/Egg Grading. | Clear Egg |
| **Clear Egg** | An infertile egg identified during candling. | Hatchery metric. | Candler, Fertility |
| **Egg Grader** | Machine that sorts table eggs by weight and checks for defects. | Layer/Packhouse. | Egg Weight |
| **Ventilation Controller** | Computer system automating fans, heaters, and inlets based on sensors. | EC House brain. | Thermostat |
| **Thermostat** | Sensor for temperature control. | Environment management. | Ventilation Controller |
| **Hygrometer** | Sensor for humidity measurement. | Crucial for incubators and EC houses. | Ventilation Controller |
| **Generator** | Backup power supply. | Critical infrastructure (power failure kills flocks). | Alarm System |
| **Alarm System** | Alerts managers to power failures or temperature spikes. | Risk management. | Generator |
| **Medicator / Dosatron** | Water-powered pump that injects precise doses of medicine into drinking water. | Health administration. | Water Vaccination |
| **Lux Meter** | Device to measure light intensity. | Layer/Breeder lighting programs. | Lighting Program |
| **Lighting Program** | A strict schedule of light hours and intensity to stimulate egg production. | Layer/Breeder management. | Lux Meter |
| **Scalder** | Tank of hot water in processing plant used to loosen feathers. | Processing. | Plucker |
