# Egg Business Chain (Poultry ERP)

## 1. Overview
The egg business chain covers two distinct flows depending on the type of eggs:
1. **Commercial Table Eggs**: Produced by Layer birds for human consumption.
2. **Hatching Eggs (HE)**: Produced by Breeder birds to be incubated for chicks.

This document outlines the end-to-end business process for both.

## 2. Table Egg Chain (Commercial Layers)
### A. Production
- **Flock Lifecycle**: Point of Lay (approx. 16-18 weeks) to Depletion (approx. 72-80 weeks).
- **Daily Operations**: Feeding, lighting, climate control, health monitoring.
- **Egg Collection**: Manual or automated belt collection. Recorded daily per shed/flock. Data points: total eggs, broken, dirty, soft-shelled.

### B. Grading & Packing
- **Transfer**: Eggs moved from shed to central grading room.
- **Sorting**: Dirty, cracked, and peewee eggs separated as downgrades.
- **Grading**: Weighed and sorted by size (Jumbo, Large, Medium, Small).
- **Packing**: Packed into trays (usually 30 eggs) and then into cartons/boxes.
- **Inventory Entry**: Finished goods inventory updated with graded egg stock.

### C. Storage
- **Conditions**: Stored in cool rooms to maintain freshness.
- **Rotation**: Strict FIFO to prevent staleness.

### D. Sales & Distribution
- **Order Processing**: Orders received from retailers/wholesalers.
- **Dispatch**: Loaded onto delivery vehicles.
- **Invoicing**: Billed per carton/tray.
- **Returns**: Minimal, but broken eggs in transit may result in credit notes.

## 3. Hatching Egg Chain (Breeders)
### A. Production
- **Flock Lifecycle**: Breeder rearing -> Production phase. Requires both male and female birds (specific mating ratio).
- **Egg Collection**: Frequent collection required. Eggs must be clean and nest-laid (floor eggs often rejected).
- **Selection**: Undersized, oversized, misshapen, or cracked eggs are rejected as hatching eggs (sold as commercial culls).

### B. Fumigation & Storage
- **Sanitization**: Eggs are fumigated or sanitized immediately after collection.
- **Cold Storage**: Stored at specific temperatures (e.g., 15-18°C) and humidity to suspend embryonic development.
- **Holding Time**: Ideally 3-7 days. Hatchability drops significantly after 7 days.

### C. Transfer to Hatchery
- **Dispatch**: Transported in specialized climate-controlled vehicles to the hatchery.
- **Receipt**: GRN generated at hatchery, eggs moved into hatchery inventory (Cold Room).

### D. Incubation & Hatching (Transition to Chick Chain)
- **Setting**: Eggs moved to setters (incubators).
- **Candling**: Checked for fertility at day 18. Infertiles removed.
- **Transfer**: Moved to hatchers.
- **Hatch**: Day 21 (for chickens).

## 4. Key Metrics & KPIs
- **Hen-Day Egg Production (HDEP)**: % of birds laying per day.
- **Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) per Dozen Eggs**: kg feed / dozen eggs.
- **% Hatchability**: (Chicks hatched / Total eggs set) * 100.
- **% Fertility**: (Fertile eggs / Total eggs set) * 100.
